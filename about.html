Built Simply. On Purpose.

IsoWatt makes portable LiFePO4 power systems engineered for real-world use. No compromises. No mass production. Every unit assembled by hand in Bellefonte, PA.

My Story

IsoWatt started as a personal need for reliable, portable power for work and play in remote locations. Existing options were either underpowered, overpriced, or came with compromises that didn't fit the job. So we built our own.

What began as a one-off project evolved into a business. We realized other people needed what we had built — systems that just work, built with quality components, and backed by someone who actually understands the product.

Why LiFePO4?

LiFePO4 chemistry is what industrial power systems use. It's stable, safe, and lasts. 2,000+ charge cycles. No thermal runaway or overnight failures. It costs more upfront, but it's the chemistry we trust with our own equipment.

Built to Order

We don't keep inventory. We don't mass-produce. Every unit is assembled, wired, and load-tested by hand before it ships. That means longer lead times, but it also means quality control that's personal.

Local Support

You're not talking to a call center, issues get escalated to the owner. We prioritize quality that doesn't stop at customer service.
